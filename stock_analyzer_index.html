How to read signals

Basic rules, not automatic instructions.
Positive trend

Price above EMA20 and EMA20 above EMA50. For long-term holdings, also check valuation and business fundamentals.

Overbought watch

RSI 14 ≥ 70. This is not automatically a sell signal; strong trends can remain overbought.

Near support

Price near the entered support level. Support can fail; wait for confirmation and define risk.

Review thesis

Price below EMA50 for a long-term position. Reassess the investment case rather than selling mechanically.
